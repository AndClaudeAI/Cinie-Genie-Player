About Us

A summer school with its beans in the right place

Sunnyside Summer School was founded on a simple theory: children learn better when they're not hangry. So while other schools hand out worksheets and hope for the best, we hand out worksheets and a small mountain of akara. It's the least a growing mind can ask for.

Our kitchen team beats, whips, and fries with the same discipline our teachers bring to the classroom — no shortcuts, no soggy batter, no compromise. The result is a campus that smells like ambition and fried black-eyed peas by 8am sharp.

  • Small class sizes, big appetites accommodated
  • Certified teachers, certifiably good akara
  • Morning custard so smooth it should have a diploma
  • Snack breaks that double as a life lesson in patience
